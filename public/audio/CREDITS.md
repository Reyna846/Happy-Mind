# Sound recording credits

The two original recordings are bundled without editing. Playback gain/loop crossfading is applied at runtime. These recordings and any adaptations remain under CC BY-SA 3.0; the licence does not apply to unrelated application code.

- **Water drops dripping** — ZooFari, 24 January 2010. Water dripping into a bowl. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Water_drops_dripping.ogg . Original: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/1c/Water_drops_dripping.ogg . Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ . Local file: `water-drops.ogg`.
- **Sound of rain** — Effib, 12 May 2014. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sound_of_rain.ogg . Original: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8a/Sound_of_rain.ogg . Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ . Local file: `rain.ogg`.

Room tone and slow-shore audio are original procedural synthesis. No recording is claimed to guarantee a calming effect. If this browser cannot decode a recording, playback falls back to generated shore noise with a notice.
